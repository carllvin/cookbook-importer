Maintenance tools

These work directly on your existing Tandoor data, independent of importing a new cookbook. Every tool shows its suggestions first - nothing is changed until you approve it.

Open suggestions

🆕

Process new recipes

For recipes added since the last run (via Tandoor or this app): translates them automatically, then suggests revising their structure (steps, ingredients per step, servings, times), matching their ingredients, units and tags to existing ones, filling in ingredient details, and adding tags.

🥕

Ingredients: review & clean up

Fixes prepared-form names, translates, and merges duplicates.

🥗

Ingredients: plural, nutrition & category

Adds a missing plural, rough nutritional values (per 100 g) and supermarket category (existing ones only) to ingredients that lack them.

⚖️

Ingredients: gram conversions

Adds the unit conversions (e.g. 1 EL olive oil = 13 g) Tandoor needs to calculate recipe nutrition - for ingredients with nutrition values.

🏷️

Tags: translate & simplify

Translates tags and merges ones that mean the same thing - in one pass.

🍂

Tags: add season

Backfills a season tag on recipes missing one.

➕

Tags: suggest more

Suggests descriptive tags (e.g. American, sweet, baking) – no ingredients. Prefers existing tags but may add useful new ones.

📏

Units: review & clean up

Unifies spellings, translates, and fills in missing plurals.

🌐

Recipes: translate

Translates existing recipes (title, description, steps, ingredient notes) into your output language.

🧩

Recipes: revise structure

Finds recipes with all ingredients in step 1 or one long block of text, and suggests splitting them into steps, assigning ingredients to steps, and filling in servings and times.
